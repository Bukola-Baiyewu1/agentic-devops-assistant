Aegis

An agentic DevOps assistant that investigates an alert, cites the runbook section that applies, proposes one fix, and then stops until a named human approves it.

Explore the API Health check Source code

What happens to an alert

  1. A signed webhook delivers the alert. Unsigned or tampered alerts are rejected.
  2. The agent checks the service's health and logs, and searches the runbooks.
  3. It proposes one action (restart or scale by one replica) with a line-level runbook citation, or escalates to a human when nothing in the runbooks supports an action.
  4. A policy layer re-checks the proposal: allowed tool, strict arguments, correct service, and a citation that really supports it.
  5. Nothing runs until an approver signs in and approves that exact action with a single-use, expiring token.
  6. Every decision is audited, and any action can be rolled back with a second, separate approval.
